Test strip analysis

The urine test strip reader analyses different reagent pads to provide accurate health information using colour detection technology.

  • Glucose analysis - Detects glucose concentration.
  • Protein analysis - Detects protein in urine.
  • Ketone analysis - Measures ketone levels.
  • Blood analysis - Detects blood in urine.
  • pH analysis - Measures urine acidity or alkalinity.
  • Bilirubin analysis - Detects bilirubin concentration.
  • Nitrite analysis - Helps identify bacterial infection.
  • Leucocyte analysis - Detects white blood cells.
  • Specific gravity - Measures urine concentration.
